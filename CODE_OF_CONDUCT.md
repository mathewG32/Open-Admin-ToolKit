# Code of Conduct

We want OpenAdmin Kit to be a welcoming project for people of every background and experience level.

**Expected:** be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, critique code rather than people, and help newcomers.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, or sustained disruption.

**Reporting:** contact the maintainer privately (see the repository profile). Reports will be handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content or block participants who violate this code.
